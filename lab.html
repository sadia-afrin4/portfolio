Three things worth building by hand

Everything in a modern chip reduces to these: a transistor pair that inverts, a gate that decides, and a register that counts. All three below are live — toggle them.

CMOS inverter

Transistor level

One PMOS pulls the output high, one NMOS pulls it low, and never both at once — which is why CMOS burns almost no power while it holds a value.
