About

Engineering research, from the transistor upward.

A graduate of the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at BRAC University (BSRM School of Engineering), where I completed a B.Sc. with a CGPA of 3.71 alongside three years of teaching, mentoring and student leadership.

My published work sits at the intersection of power systems, machine learning and security: intrusion detection for smart-grid communication, wide-area monitoring, next-generation energy storage, and digital-twin methods for electric-vehicle charging. Six articles, all in Q1 journals, with first authorship on the first of them.

Underneath that applied work is a longer-standing interest in the hardware itself — VLSI design, semiconductor devices and analogue and digital circuit design — carried through Verilog, VHDL and the Cadence toolchain. I am now seeking an M.Sc. at a leading UK university to take that interest further.
